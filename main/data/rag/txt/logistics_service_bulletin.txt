NORTHWIND LOGISTICS - SERVICE BULLETIN 2026/07

Effective from 1 July 2026, the standard delivery commitment for domestic
shipments is reduced from 72 hours to 48 hours. Shipments classified as
oversized, defined as any single item exceeding 180 centimetres on its longest
side, remain on the 72 hour commitment.

Refrigerated storage is billed at 4.50 euros per pallet per day. The first
48 hours of refrigerated storage after arrival are free of charge, provided the
consignment is collected within that window.

A delay exceeding two hours on a scheduled collection triggers a penalty of
120 euros payable to the customer. The penalty is applied automatically and
does not require a claim to be filed.

The company reduced its transport emissions by 14 per cent during the 2025
financial year, measured against the 2023 baseline. The reduction is attributed
mainly to the replacement of 38 diesel vans with electric equivalents across
the northern depots.

Temperature records produced by data loggers must be retained for 5 years.
Records may be stored electronically provided the storage medium is audited
annually.

The night shift surcharge applies between 22:00 and 06:00 and amounts to
18 per cent of the base tariff. It does not apply to collections rescheduled at
the request of Northwind.

For the Salamanca to Valladolid corridor the current tariff is 0.92 euros per
kilometre plus a fixed handling fee of 45 euros per consignment.
